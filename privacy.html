Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Your privacy is important to me. This policy outlines how I handle your information when you visit lewiswake.com.

1. Website Analytics & Tracking

To help me improve the performance and design of this website, I partner with Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site. This service captures behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replays (such as how you scroll, click, and move your mouse).

Website usage data is captured using first and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies. I use this information solely for site optimization and to ensure the website is performing at its best. No personally identifiable information (such as your name or email) is linked to these session recordings.

For more detailed information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, please review the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

2. Embedded Content & External Links

This website serves primarily as a hub to link you to my content on other platforms (such as YouTube, Patreon, Bandcamp, Spotify, etc.) and sometimes embeds their players directly onto the page.

Embedded Content: Articles or pages on this site (such as the Hallowake page) include embedded content (e.g., YouTube videos, Bandcamp players). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if you had visited the other website directly. These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content, especially if you have an account and are logged in to that website.

External Links: Once you click links and leave lewiswake.com, you are subject to the privacy policies of those respective third-party websites.

3. Contact Form & Personal Data

If you use the contact form on this website to get in touch regarding bookings or inquiries:

  • Data Collected: I collect only the information you voluntarily provide (Name, Email Address, and Message).
  • Processing: The contact form is powered securely by Formspree. When you submit the form, your data is processed through their servers before being delivered securely to my inbox.
  • Purpose & Confidentiality: This information is used solely to reply to your inquiry. Your details are strictly confidential. I will never sell, share, or distribute your email address to third parties or marketing lists.

4. Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in how the site operates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact me via the form on the homepage.
